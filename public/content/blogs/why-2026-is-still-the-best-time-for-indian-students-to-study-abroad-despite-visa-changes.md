## FAQs

**Q1. Which country is best for Indian students in 2026?**

It depends on your goals* Canada is strong for PR pathways, the UK for shorter courses, Germany for affordability, and Ireland for tech careers*

**Q2. Is studying abroad still worth it in 2026?**

Yes* International education provides global exposure, better career opportunities, and strong earning potential*

**Q3. Which countries have high visa success rates for Indian students?**

Countries like the UK, Ireland, Germany, and New Zealand currently show strong opportunities for genuine students with proper documentation*

**Q4. What are the most in-demand courses abroad?**

Courses in IT, Data Science, AI, Healthcare, Cybersecurity, Business Analytics, and Engineering are highly in demand*

**Q5. How early should students apply for September intake?**

Ideally 6–10 months before intake to secure university admission, visa processing, and scholarships*

**Q6. Can students study abroad without IELTS?**

Some universities accept alternatives like MOI, interviews, or internal English tests depending on the country and institution*

**Q7. What is the biggest reason for student visa refusal?**

Weak SOPs, unclear career goals, insufficient financial proof, and inconsistent academic profiles are common reasons*
